What is growth hack ?

"Growth hacking" is a term that refers to a marketing strategy Reviewed on rapid growth, particularly for startups. It involves using creative, innovative, and often low-cost methods to acquire and retain customers, leveraging data and analytics to optimize marketing efforts. The concept emphasizes the importance of experimentation and iteration to identify the most effective growth strategies, rather than relying solely on traditional marketing techniques.

In essence, growth hacking is about finding efficient ways to grow a business quickly and sustainably by utilizing a mix of marketing, product management, and data science techniques. This approach is particularly popular in tech startups where the ability to achieve viral growth can be pivotal to success.
